- Take rate (30%): Direct measure of margin extraction
- Revenue stability (20%): Year-over-year consistency

- Growth potential (15%): Market size, expansion room
- Risk profile (10%): Adjustment concentration, dependence

## IV. Answering the 7 Questions

### Q1: ¿Cuál marketplace deja más utilidad?

**RIPLEY ($63.3M)** is the most profitable 3rd-party marketplace. PARIS ($378.1M) is larger but 1st party.

### Q2: ¿Cuál marketplace destruye más margen?

**ML destroys $306.6M** through adjustments — 90.3% of gross charges returned. For every $1 charged, $0.90 is credited back.

### Q3: ¿Qué cobros debemos renegociar primero?

1. **ML adjustment policies** — $52M potential if return rate drops from 90.3% to 75%
2. **Logistics rates** — $10.5M via volume consolidation
3. **Full storage pricing** — $1.5M via tiered pricing

### Q4: ¿Qué categorías son más rentables?

Due to data limitations (no category dimension), we analyzed at concept level:
- **Comisiones** (all MPs): Core profit engine — $159M net
- **Publicidad ML**: Highest margin — $48M with near-zero cost
- **Logística**: Cost center — but RIPLEY generates margin here

### Q5: ¿Dónde debemos crecer?

**RIPLEY.** Highest quality revenue. Each $1M of RIPLEY GMV = $179K in revenue vs $37K for ML.

### Q6: ¿Dónde debemos reducir exposición?

**ML.** 49.6% of total GMV passes through ML but only 6.4% of revenue comes from it. If adjustment policies forced 10% tighter, ML would become unprofitable.

### Q7: ¿Cuánto dinero adicional podemos recuperar sin vender más?

| Scenario | Recovery | Actions required |
|---|---|---|
| **Conservative** | **$10.3M** | 5% logistics rate renegotiation + BPP policy tweak |
| **Realistic** | **$46.0M** | 10-15% reductions on negotiable categories |
| **Optimistic** | **$143.1M** | 30% across all opportunities |

**Realistic target: $46M additional annual recovery without selling one more product.**
