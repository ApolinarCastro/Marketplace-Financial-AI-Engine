From Eccsa's perspective, "recovery" means:
- **For concepts Eccsa PAYS** (logistics, adjustments): reduce the rate
- **For concepts Eccsa RECEIVES** (ads, subscriptions): these are seller costs, Eccsa wants to grow or maintain them

However, from the SELLER's perspective (the analysis goal), "recovery" means reducing seller costs.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| ML adjustments (net positive for sellers) | $306,606,250 | $30,660,625 | $61,321,250 | $91,981,875 |

Wait — adjustments are CREDITS to sellers. Reducing them would HURT sellers. Let me reconsider.

Actually, the adjustments are a complex area. They include:
1. BPP refunds ($94M): Buyer protection — marketplace refunds sellers when claims are valid
2. Quality adjustments ($98M): Size/color/delivery issues — marketplace credits sellers
3. Reconciliation credits ($41M): System-level corrections

If Eccsa reduces these adjustments (tougher policies), sellers would LOSE this money. But if Eccsa maintains the current policies, sellers keep getting these credits.

From Eccsa's perspective, the adjustments are a cost (money going back to sellers). Reducing adjustments would increase Eccsa's margin but hurt sellers.

Let me re-frame: the adjustments are NOT "savings" from a seller perspective. They are protections. A seller wants HIGHER adjustments (more credits back), not lower.

## III. Priority Negotiation Targets

### Target 1: ML Adjustments ($306.6M)

**Current state:** ML returns 90.3% of gross charges as adjustments. Net take rate = 3.7%.

**Opportunity:** If Eccsa reduces adjustment rate from 90.3% to 80% (tightening BPP policy, quality thresholds):

| Current gross charges | Current adjustments | New adjustments | Net gain to Eccsa |
|---|---|---|---|
| $339,400,328 | $306,606,250 | $271,520,262 | **+$35,086,000** |

This would increase ML's net take rate from 3.7% to 7.8%.

**Risk:** Sellers would complain about reduced buyer protection. Competitive disadvantage.

### Target 2: RIPLEY Acuerdo Comercial ($18.4M)

**Current state:** XML shows $18.4M as "MKP Acuerdo Comercial" but ledger absorbs it. Sellers cannot see it.

**Opportunity:** Renegotiate with RIPLEY to unbundle — seller sees separate lines:

| Action | Potential recovery |
|---|---|
| Make visible (no $ impact) | $0 direct, improved transparency |
| Renegotiate rate down | Depends on contract |

**Conclusion:** Transparency improvement, no immediate $ recovery.

### Target 3: Publicidad ML ($47.9M)

**Current state:** Sellers spend $47.9M on ML ads. This is revenue for ML (and by extension, for Eccsa as operator).

**Opportunity:** Optimize ad spend — not reduce it. Better targeting, higher ROAS.

| Action | Impact on sellers | Impact on Eccsa |
|---|---|---|
| Reduce ad spend | +$47.9M seller savings | -$47.9M Eccsa revenue |
| Optimize ROAS | Better efficiency | Same/stable |

**Conclusion:** Ad optimization benefits both parties.

### Target 4: Logística Volumes ($105.1M)
